# motuorcycle-maintenance-tracker

A maintenance tracker for motorcyclists who'd rather not rely on "I think I changed the oil at some point."

## why did i make this
a lot of people in my life ride a bike - i've seen them go on their everyday commute, short and long rides, accidents, and spend a lot of money because certain things stop working (and they don't even know why sometimes). this is an homage to them - cheers to adam, chris, and tiffany. 

## what it does (V1)
- shows every tracked component's status at a glance: OK, Due soon, Overdue, or Unverified
- calculates status from distance ridden, time since last service, and the manufacturer's schedule
- sends reminders before things become overdue
- logs checks and services in under 30 seconds
- handles second-hand bikes with unknown history
- works offline

## what it doesn't do
it is not a diagnostic tool. it won't predict component lifespans, book mechanics, or replace a professional.

## starting bike
v1 targets the Yamaha XVS650 (V-Star 650), with intervals verified against the factory owner's manual.
