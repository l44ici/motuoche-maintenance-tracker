# Use Cases

These scenarios are based on real people and real riding habits.

## A notification stops a small problem becoming a big one
A rider who commutes most days gets a notification that the engine oil is due for a check. He opens the app, sees it's been almost 5,000 km since the last change, and books it in that week. Without the reminder, it would have gone unnoticed for another month.

## Logging a check takes less than a minute
A rider who does his own basic maintenance checks his tyre pressure and brake fluid on a Sunday morning, then logs both in the app. It takes about 20 seconds. The status resets and he knows when to look at them again.

## A quick check before a long ride
A rider with a big weekend ride planned opens the app the night before. Most things are fine, but the brake pads are showing as overdue. He checks them, finds they're getting thin, and replaces them before the ride rather than halfway through it.

## Setting up a second-hand bike
A rider has just bought a used motorcycle with an unknown service history. During setup, he enters the current odometer reading and skips the components he's unsure about. The app marks those as unverified and flags them straight away, so he knows what to get checked before relying on the bike.

## Knowing what's involved before visiting a mechanic
A rider sees her spark plugs flagged as due. She's never dealt with spark plugs and isn't sure if it's a big job. The app shows a note on what's involved, so she goes to the mechanic already knowing what to expect.
